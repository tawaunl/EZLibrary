Everything EZLibrary does

Twelve workspaces over one Serato library. Each one reads and writes the same files, takes a snapshot before it changes anything, and never guesses on your behalf.

Tracks & Tags

Browse the whole library, fix metadata in place, and fill thousands of empty fields in one pass.

Tag Verification

Cross-check your tags against several music databases and AI, and only change what the evidence agrees on.

Audio Editor

Trim dead air, long intros and unwanted tails off a track — without re-encoding it.

Find Duplicates

Catch the same song saved five times, even when every copy is named differently.

Missing Tracks

Find every file Serato has lost track of, and relink it to where it actually lives.

Crates

Your whole crate tree, including smart crates and the hidden ones, with real stats.

PlaylistMatch

Paste a playlist, find out what you already own, and get a crate out of it.

Add Music

Get new files into your library and into the right crate, without the drag-and-drop dance.

Library Consolidation

Pull music scattered across drives and folders into one place, without breaking a single crate.

Backup

Timestamped snapshots of your library, sized before you commit to them.

Rename From Tags

Consistent filenames across your whole library — and Serato still finds every one of them.

Command Line Tools

The same engine, scriptable — for imports, bulk repair and anything you want to automate.
Across every feature

The rules that don't change

  • A timestamped snapshot before every write, whether or not you asked for a backup.
  • Atomic writes, so an interrupted operation cannot leave a Serato file half-written.
  • Read-back verification after tag writes, with a rollback if what landed does not match.
  • Refuses to write while Serato is running, because Serato would overwrite the change on quit.
  • Nothing repaired silently — ambiguous cases are reported to you, not guessed at.
  • Cues, beat grids and play counts survive renames, moves and repairs.
